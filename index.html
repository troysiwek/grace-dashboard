Agentic commerce has crossed from "brands piloting assistants" to "the platform layer going generally available underneath them" — the shopping rails are commoditizing, so the value moves up to the brand-owned build and governance on top, which is exactly BrightSol's lane

Salesforce shipped Agentforce Commerce — Shopper, Buyer and Merchant agents now generally available and able to act (check inventory, confirm cutoff, close the sale), with native ChatGPT / Google / Gemini hooks — and ChatGPT Shopping opened to all U.S. users (Etsy + 1M+ Shopify merchants), while Google's AI Mode added agentic checkout (Wayfair, Chewy, Etsy) atop its Universal Commerce Protocol. Both sit on open rails now — OpenAI's Agentic Commerce Protocol (with Stripe; Instacart, DoorDash, Shopify, Etsy) and Google's UCP. When the checkout rails become a commodity every platform hands out, the differentiator is no longer having an agent — it's owning the one on your own surface: who governs it, what it reads, how it's maintained as catalog and tools drift. Salesforce's own number — retailers running their own shopper agents grew sales ~59% faster — is the "move now" case for a client on the fence, and McKinsey's $900B–$1T U.S. agentic-commerce forecast by 2030 is the size of the pond. The build-plus-maintain engagement (owner card, permissions, executable trust checks, a maintenance loop) is the retainer BrightSol is built to sell.
